**Reading.**
- **Snow holds throughput flat** at about 4.6 jobs/s from 90 to 180 agents. Its message cost per
  job stays constant at about 170, and its p50 wait grows only as the queue does: 20 jobs per agent
  at each size.
- **PBFT's message cost per job grows with fleet size:** about 3.4× from 90 to 180 agents, the
  all-to-all cost. Its throughput halves at each step.
  - At 180 agents it completes 84 % within the hour.
  - The plan defines "collapse" as below 50 % completion, which this does not reach. **Write
    "livelocks and degrades severely", not "stops completing".**

## 4. E5 ablations beside their E1′ references

**Read this first: the references are older code.**
- The Hier-90 references come from the E1′ campaign at c125ed8c (`../e0-e1-c125ed8c`).
- The Hier-270 references come from ed8c9101 (`../e1-hier270-ed8c9101`).
- The E5 arms ran at isc27-freeze.
- Code that changed in between: the PBFT counter margin, voting on adoption, `SCAN_STATES`, and
  the tier-sized vote stash.

The comparisons below are indicative. The frozen re-run supplies same-revision references, and
only those are citable.

**Gossip off (Hier-90): no measurable effect** on either engine. Every metric is within one std
of its reference. Examples:
- PBFT job p95: 50.5 ± 4.7 vs 54.0 ± 3.3 s.
- Snow job p95: 51.7 ± 0.8 vs 54.4 ± 3.0 s.
- Snow coordinator decision p50: 1.22 vs 1.23 s.

At Hier-90 under the utilization cost model, the peer load that gossip carries doesn't change any
decision measurably.

**Peers (`coordinator_cost_matrix: peers`) trades coordinator decision time for placement
quality.** With peers, each job gets about one proposer at the coordinator tier (1.0–1.1),
against 5.6–27 with `self`. Coordinator decisions get faster, and everything downstream gets
slower:

| | coord p50 (s) | arrival→assigned p95 (s) | job p95 (s) | makespan (s) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hier-90 PBFT, self → peers | 0.64 → 0.08 | 9.3 → 49.4 | 54.0 → 81.4 | 232 → 232 |
| Hier-90 Snow, self → peers | 1.23 → 1.00 | 10.6 → 191.3 | 54.4 → 210.4 | 224 → 363 |
| Hier-270 PBFT, self → peers | 1.56 → 0.29 | 10.6 → 160.9 | 60.1 → 186.8 | 408 → 498 |
| Hier-270 Snow, self → peers | 68.3 → 1.08 | 138.0 → 463.7 | 158.8 → 484.3 | 527 → 764 |

- **Why downstream gets slower:** under `peers` the single proposer prices every candidate group
  from its own view and picks one, so jobs pile up behind that choice.
  - Snow under peers drops Jain across leaves (0.54 → 0.42 at Hier-90).
  - Snow's throughput falls by 30–40 %.
- **Hier-270 Snow** shows the sharpest contrast. Its 68 s coordinator decision p50 under `self`,
  with 27 coordinators all proposing, collapses to 1.1 s under peers. Job latency still triples.
- **Conclusion:** as `CLAUDE.md` says, `peers` is an E5 arm, not a fix. It removes the
  coordinator-tier contention and moves the cost into queueing.
